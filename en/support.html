Account

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings › Account › Delete account (Ajustes › Cuenta › Eliminar cuenta) and confirm. It’s deleted straight away. If you can’t get in, email hola@pista.one with the subject “Delete account”. All the details are on this page.

Heads up: deleting your account doesn’t cancel Pista+. If you’re subscribed, cancel it with Apple first.

I need a break. Can I hide my profile?

Yes. In Settings (Ajustes) you can pause your profile: nobody new will see it, but you keep your matches and chats. Switch it back on whenever you’re ready.

I can’t sign in

Pista uses Sign in with Apple and Continue with Google, so you need to use the same Apple or Google account you signed up with. If it still doesn’t work, check your iPhone is online and that date and time are set automatically. If you still can’t get in, email us.

How do I change my city or location?

Pista uses your iPhone’s approximate location. If you’ve moved, open the app with location turned on and it updates by itself. You can check the permission in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Pista.

Pista+ and purchases

How do I cancel Pista+?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone:

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Go to Subscriptions and choose Pista.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Do it at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep Pista+ until the end of the period you’ve already paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are taken by Apple, so refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com. We don’t have access to your payments.

I paid and it isn’t showing

Make sure you’re signed in to Pista with the same Apple account you bought with, and use the app’s restore purchases option. If it still doesn’t show, email us with the date of purchase and the product, but no card details.

Likes, Aces and Boosts

How many likes do I get?

Without Pista+ you get likes every day, which reset at midnight Spanish mainland time. With Pista+ they’re unlimited. You can like a whole profile or something specific, like a photo, an answer or an achievement, and add a comment.

What’s an Ace?

A highlighted like, for when you really like someone. You get one free Ace a week, three with Pista+, and you can buy more in packs of 3 or 10.

What’s a Boost?

For 30 minutes your profile shows up among the first for people in your area. Pista+ includes one Boost a week, and you can buy more singly or in a pack of 5. Open the app seven days in a row and you earn one too.

Waitlist

When do I get in?

We open city by city, starting with Madrid, and in batches, for quality control: we want the app to be full of people who suit you when you get in. We’ll email you when it’s your turn, with a code to go straight in. You can check your place with the private link we sent you when you joined.

Can I get in sooner?

Yes, with an invite. Know someone who’s already in? Ask them for their invite and skip the wait. It’s the only way past the waitlist: sharing your link doesn’t move you up. If you’ve already got a code, open the link you were sent or type it in at pista.one.

I haven’t had the confirmation email

Check promotions and spam. If it isn’t there, join again with the same email at pista.one/en/waitlist and we’ll send it again.

How do I leave the waitlist?

Email us at hola@pista.one from the address you joined with and we’ll remove you.

Invites

How do invites work?

Once you’re in, you’ll find them under Profile, Invites: 5 in your first week and then 3 every week, topped up on Mondays (they don’t roll over). Whoever joins with yours skips the waitlist and, after a few days with a complete profile, you both get a Boost: your profile shows up first in your area for a while.

Someone sent me an invite. What now?

Open the link on your iPhone, download Pista and type the code under Got an invite or a code? Each invite works for one person and expires after a few days, so don’t sit on it.

Safety

How do I report someone?

Open the menu on their profile or in the chat, tap Report (Denunciar), choose the reason and tell us what happened. We review every report and the other person won’t know it was you. If you’re in danger, call 112.

What happens when I block someone?

You’ll never see each other anywhere in the app again: not in the feed, likes or chat. If you’d matched, the match is removed.

I’m not getting notifications

Check they’re allowed in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Pista, and that you don’t have a Focus mode silencing them.

Can’t find what you need?

Email us and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can.
