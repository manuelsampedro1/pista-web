How it works

From profile to first date in three moves.

  1. 01

    Your sport, up front

    Every profile shows what they play, at what level and when they train. You can tell at a glance if you'll get on.

  2. 02

    Like something specific

    A photo or an answer, with a comment if you like. It's a lot easier to get talking that way than with a hi.

  3. 03

    Suggest a plan

    When you match, you suggest the date from the chat: sport, place and time. One tap to accept and you've got a plan.

What people usually ask.

What is Pista?

A dating app for iPhone made for people who play sport. Every profile shows their sports, level and when they train, and when you match you can suggest a first date doing something you both enjoy.

When can I get in?

We're starting in Madrid and opening in groups, so there are people near you when you get in. Join the waitlist and we'll email you. If someone already inside invites you, you go straight in.

What if I don't live in Madrid?

Join anyway and pick your city. Barcelona and Valencia are next, and we'll email you as soon as yours opens.

How do you keep it safe?

Every account verifies its phone by text, it's 18 and over only, and nobody sees your date of birth or where you live: just your age and how many kilometres away you are. You can report or block from any profile or chat, and we review every report within 24 hours. It's all in the community guidelines.

Is it free?

Yes. For free you get 12 likes a day and one Ace a week, and chatting and suggesting dates is free. Pista+ adds unlimited likes, seeing who liked you and filters by sport, level and schedule.

Do I need to be really sporty?

Not at all. Some people compete and some go to the gym twice a week when work allows. Be honest about your level and you'll find people at your pace.

Can I use it to find a training partner?

Pista is a dating app. Sport is what you have in common and the perfect excuse for a first date, but people are here to meet someone. That said, for padel, tennis or Hyrox Doubles you can say you're looking for a partner.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, Account, Delete account. If you can't get in, here's how.
