FAQ

What people ask us.

What is Pista?

A dating app for iPhone made for people who play sport. Every profile shows their sports, level and when they train, and when you match you can suggest a first date doing something you both enjoy.

When can I get in?

We're starting with Madrid and opening in batches, for quality control: we want the app to be full of people who suit you when you get in. We'll email you when it's your turn. If someone already inside invites you, you go straight in.

How do I move up the list?

With your link. For every person who joins with it and confirms their email, you move up 50 places and so do they. Tell us your gender, who you're looking for and your sports and you move up another 25.

What if I don't live in Madrid?

Join anyway and pick your city. We'll email you as soon as yours opens.

Is it free?

Yes. For free you get likes every day and one Ace a week, and chatting and suggesting dates is free. Pista+ adds unlimited likes, seeing who likes you and filters by sport, level and schedule.

Do I need to be really sporty?

Not at all. Some people compete and some go to the gym twice a week when work allows. Be honest about your level and you'll find people at your pace.

How do you keep it safe?

Every account verifies its phone by text, it's 18 and over only, and nobody sees your date of birth or where you live: just your age and how many kilometres away you are. You can get selfie verified, and report or block from any profile or chat. We review every report within 24 hours. It's all in the community guidelines.

Can I use it to find a training partner?

Pista is a dating app. Sport is what you have in common and the perfect excuse for a first date, but people are here to meet someone. That said, for padel, tennis or Hyrox Doubles you can say you're looking for a partner.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, Account, Delete account. If you can't get in, here's how. To leave the waitlist, email us at hola@pista.one.
