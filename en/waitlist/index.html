    Questions

    Why a waitlist?

    An empty dating app is no use to anyone. We open city by city and in groups, so there are people near you and at your level when you get in.

    When is my turn?

    We're starting with Madrid. It depends on your city and your place: the first in each group get in first, and sharing your link moves you up.

    Can I skip the wait?

    Yes. With an invite from someone already inside, or the code of a Pista ambassador, you go straight in.

    What do you do with my email?

    We use it to confirm your spot and tell you when it's your turn. We don't sell it or use it for advertising. The details are in the privacy policy.

    Why do you ask if I'm a man or a woman?

    To open balanced groups: a dating app that's almost all guys doesn't work for anyone. It's optional and we only use it for that.
