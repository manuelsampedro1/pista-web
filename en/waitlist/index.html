How you get in

  1. Join

    Your name, your email and your city. Confirm from the email and your spot is saved.

  2. Or ask for an invite

    If you know someone who's already in, ask them for theirs and skip the wait.

  3. You're up

    We open in batches, for quality control. You'll get an email with your code and go straight into the app.

Questions

Why is there a waitlist?

An empty dating app is no use to anyone. We open city by city and in batches, for quality control, so there are people near you who suit you when you get in.

When is my turn?

We're starting with Madrid. We'll email you when your batch opens.

Can I skip the wait?

Yes, with an invite from someone who's already in: everyone on Pista gets a few every week. A Pista ambassador's code gets you straight in too.

What do you do with my data?

We use your name and email to save your spot, confirm your email and tell you when it's your turn. We don't sell them or use them for advertising. It's all in the privacy policy.
