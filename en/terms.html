We can’t promise you matches, dates or that a particular person will reply. We’ll keep improving the app and may change, add or remove features. If a change significantly affects something you’ve paid for, we’ll tell you in advance.

We do our best to keep Pista running, but there may be downtime for maintenance, technical faults or reasons beyond our control.

10. Pista+, Boosts and Aces

Pista is free to use. You can also buy, within the app:

  • Pista+, an auto-renewing subscription, for periods of one week, one month or three months.
  • Boosts, which put your profile among the first in your area for 30 minutes.
  • Aces, likes that arrive highlighted.

What each product includes and its price, taxes included, are shown in the app before you buy.

Billing and renewal

All purchases are charged by Apple to your Apple account when you confirm them, under the App Store terms. Pista+ renews automatically at the end of each period, for the same length and at the current price, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. How to cancel

In iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Pista, or from your account in the App Store. You keep Pista+ until the end of the period you’ve already paid for. Deleting your Pista account doesn’t cancel the subscription: you have to cancel it with Apple.

If we change the price of Pista+, Apple will let you know before the renewal and, where its rules or the law require it, ask you to accept the new price.

11. Waitlist, invites and ambassadors

Waitlist

Joining the waitlist is free and gives you a place in your city’s queue. We open in batches, for quality control, so there are people who suit you when you get in. Your place is used to order the batches we let in; it isn’t a right to get in on a given date. Every person who joins with your link and confirms their email moves you both up, unless we see it’s the same person or the same connection. Whoever opens your link will see your first name. The access code we send you is personal, works once and expires.

Invites

Once you’re in, you get a number of invites each week (shown in the app). They renew on Mondays, don’t roll over, and each one expires a few days after it’s created if nobody uses it, which gives the slot back. Each invite works for one new person, who skips the waitlist.

When that person verifies their phone, completes their profile and stays on Pista for the days shown in the app, you both get the reward shown at that time (for example, a Boost, an Ace or extra likes). Rewards have no monetary value, can’t be exchanged for money or transferred, and we may change the amount for future invites.

Ambassadors

If you join with a Pista ambassador’s code or link, you go straight in and that ambassador may receive a commission from Pista. You pay nothing for it and the ambassador never sees your details. The ambassador terms are on a separate page.

Abuse

If we spot abuse, such as fake or duplicate accounts, inviting yourself or deleting accounts to claim a reward again, we may hold or remove the rewards and close those accounts. When something looks suspicious, a person reviews it before deciding.
