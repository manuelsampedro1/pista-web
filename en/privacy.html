The short version

  • We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Your data is stored on servers in the European Union, in Dublin (Ireland).
  • Who you want to meet can reveal your sexual orientation. We only use it with your explicit consent.
  • We ask for a phone number verified by text message so that everyone on Pista is real. Nobody else sees it.
  • If you join the waitlist, we only email you to confirm your address and to tell you when your group opens.
  • You can delete your account from the app at any time: Settings › Account › Delete account.
  • This website uses no cookies or trackers.

Special category data

Some Pista data falls into the special categories of Article 9 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Data that can reveal your sex life or sexual orientation. The genders of the people you want to meet, together with your own gender and what you’re looking for, can reveal your sexual orientation. Using a dating app also says something about your personal life.

We only process this data with your explicit consent, which we ask for in the app before collecting it. You can withdraw it at any time (section 4).

We don’t ask for other sensitive data, such as ethnic origin, religion, political opinions or health. If you choose to mention something like that on your profile or in a message, bear in mind other people will see it.

4. Consent and how to withdraw it

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it and doesn’t affect anything we did with it before.

  • Gender preferences and data about your orientation. These are essential for showing you profiles. To withdraw this consent you need to delete your account.
  • Location. In iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Pista. Without location we can’t work out distances and the profiles you see will be less relevant.
  • Notifications. In iPhone Settings › Notifications › Pista.
  • Waitlist. Email us and we’ll take you off the list. If you don’t confirm your email within 30 days, we delete it ourselves.

5. Who else sees or handles your data

Other Pista users

Anyone who sees your profile will see your name, your age (never your date of birth), your gender if you choose to show it, your city and approximate distance in kilometres, your sports and level, schedule, what you’re looking for, answers, photos, achievements, any optional details you haven’t hidden, your Strava link if you’ve added one, whether you’ve been active recently and whether you have an active Boost.

We never show other people your exact location, your date of birth, the genders you’re interested in or your email. Messages are only visible to the people in the chat.

Profile photos are served through links that aren’t published outside the app, but anyone who has the exact link to a photo could open it.

Providers who process data on our behalf

  • Supabase, Inc. (United States): database, sign in, photo storage, server functions and real time messaging. The data is hosted in the European Union, on Amazon Web Services, region eu-west-1 (Dublin, Ireland).
  • Apple, to send you notifications through the Apple Push Notification service.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States), which hosts this website.
  • Twilio Inc. (United States), which sends the text messages with the code to verify your phone.
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., United States), which sends the waitlist and ambassador emails.
  • The email provider we use to manage hola@pista.one.

They all process data following our instructions and under a data processing agreement.

Apple as an independent controller

Sign in with Apple, the App Store and billing for in app purchases are Apple services. For those, Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd. in the European Union, and Apple Inc.) processes your data as a controller under its own privacy policy. We only receive what Apple passes on and you authorise.

Google as an independent controller

If you choose Continue with Google, Google (Google Ireland Limited in the European Union) processes the data for that sign in as a controller under its own privacy policy. We only receive what you authorise on Google’s screen.

Authorities

We share data with courts, the police or other authorities only when the law requires it or when it’s needed to protect someone’s life or physical safety.

6. International transfers

We store your data in the European Union. Even so, Supabase, Apple, Google, GitHub, Twilio and Resend are US companies, and their staff or sub-processors may access some data from outside the European Economic Area, for example to provide technical support or for security.

In those cases the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified under it and, if not, on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission, with additional safeguards where needed. You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards at hola@pista.one.

7. How long we keep your data

  • While you have an account. We keep your data while you use Pista. If you delete a photo or change something, we replace the previous version.
  • When you delete your account. We delete your profile, photos, preferences, likes, matches, messages and notification tokens from our systems straight away, and within 30 days at most they’re gone from backups too.
  • Reports. Reports and the information needed to handle them are kept for up to 12 months, even if the account is deleted, to protect the community and stop banned people from coming back with a new account. After that they’re deleted.
  • Purchases. Apple, as the seller, keeps billing records in line with its legal obligations. We keep transaction IDs while your account exists, unless we need to keep them restricted to deal with a claim or a legal obligation.
  • Waitlist. If you don’t confirm your email, we delete it after 30 days. If you do, we keep it until you get into Pista or ask us to remove you. Once you’re in, your spot is linked to your account and deleted with it.
  • Fraud signals. Network and device summaries are deleted after 90 days. When you delete your account we keep only those encrypted summaries and the dates for 90 days, so nobody can delete an account and come back to claim the same reward again. Then they’re deleted.
  • Usage events. Up to 400 days. When you delete your account they’re unlinked from you and only remain as totals.
  • Invites and commissions. The record of who invited whom and of ambassador commissions is kept without your profile data for as long as needed to reconcile payments and meet tax obligations.
  • Support emails. As long as needed to sort out your request and, after that, restricted for as long as claims could arise.

When the law requires us to keep something, we keep it restricted (“bloqueado”), as set out in Article 32 of Spain’s Organic Law 3/2018 on Personal Data Protection (LOPDGDD): set aside, not used, and only available to courts and authorities for the legal period.

8. Your rights

You can exercise these rights over your data at any time:

  • Access: find out what data we hold and get a copy.
  • Rectification: correct anything that’s wrong or incomplete.
  • Erasure: have it deleted.
  • Restriction: have us stop using it while we deal with a complaint from you.
  • Portability: get the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used format, or have us send it to another controller.
  • Objection: to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (section 4).
  • Not be subject to automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (section 10).

How to exercise them

  • In the app. You can edit your profile, hide details, pause your profile and delete your account in Settings › Account › Delete account.
  • By email. Write to hola@pista.one with the subject “Data protection” and tell us which right you want to exercise. As you sign in with Apple or Google, we may ask for some account details to check it’s really you. We’ll never ask for more than we need.

It’s free. We’ll reply within one month, which can be extended by two more months for complex requests. If that happens, we’ll let you know.

If you think we haven’t handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, C/ Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid, www.aepd.es) or to the authority in your country of residence. We’d appreciate it if you wrote to us first so we can try to put it right.

9. Under 18s

Pista is for over 18s only. We check the date of birth when the profile is created and don’t let anyone younger continue. If we know or suspect an account belongs to a minor, we close it and delete their data. If you think someone is underage, report them in the app or email us.

10. Profile order and automated decisions

To decide the order in which we show you profiles, we use automated criteria: whether someone has an active Boost, whether there’s already interest from the other person, sports in common, recent activity and distance. We also filter by your age, distance and gender preferences and, with Pista+, by sport, level and schedule.

This isn’t a decision with legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR: it only changes the order in which you see people. We don’t build profiles for advertising.

11. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and encryption at rest on our provider’s servers.
  • Row level access rules: each account can only read and change what belongs to it.
  • Location is rounded before storage, and other people only see the distance in kilometres.
  • Your date of birth, location and preferences are never sent to other users.
  • No passwords of our own: sign in happens through Apple or Google, and your phone is verified with a one-time code.
  • Fraud signals are stored as summaries encrypted with a secret key, never in clear.
  • Data relating to purchases, Boosts or Aces can’t be changed from the app.
  • Access to production data is limited to the controller.

No system is perfect. If a security breach puts you at risk, we’ll tell you and notify the Spanish Data Protection Agency within the legal deadlines.

12. This website, no cookies

pista.one uses no cookies, analytics, pixels or any other trackers, and stores nothing in your browser. Fonts are served from our own domain, so the site doesn’t connect to Google or any other third party when it loads. That’s why you don’t see a cookie banner.

Like any server, GitHub, which hosts the site, receives your IP address when you visit and may keep it in its logs for security.

If you join the waitlist, the site sends your email and city to our server, which keeps an encrypted summary of your network to stop automated sign ups. Your private link to check your place goes in the page address, not in cookies. The ambassador and admin dashboards keep your session only in that tab’s memory, which the browser clears when you close it.

If you follow a link to the App Store or Apple Maps, Apple’s privacy policy applies.

13. We don’t sell data

We don’t sell or rent your data, we don’t share it with third parties for their own advertising, and there are no ads in Pista.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change anything important, we’ll let you know in the app before it applies and, where needed, ask for your consent again. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
